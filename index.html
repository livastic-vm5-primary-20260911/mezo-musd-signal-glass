METHODOLOGY & LIMITS

This front-end issues allowlisted, read-only JSON-RPC calls for ERC-20 supply, decimals, balanceOf, allowance, and the MUSD/mUSDC pair's token0/token1/getReserves. A mismatched network, token, pair or token decimal causes an error, not a fabricated output. Ratios are computed from reserves and can differ significantly from executable exchange rates due to fees, route, slippage, depegs and stale liquidity. No financial return is suggested.
